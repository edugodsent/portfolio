About Me

I write it before i shoot it. That's the whole secret.: Most people jump straight to typing prompts and hoping something good comes out. I don't do that.. Every project starts with real writing — character bibles, dialogue, shot lists — before a single frame is generated.

That means locked characters with consistent wardrobe and voice across every scene, prompts written specifically for each tool's strengths, and a workflow that goes from creative direction to a finished, platform-ready deliverable.

Work spans original drama series, brand commercials, and podcast production — for Edugodsent's own catalogue and for client brands.
